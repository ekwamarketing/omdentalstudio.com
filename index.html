Common Questions

Porcelain Veneers FAQs

Dental veneers are wafer-thin laminates of porcelain bonded to the front-facing teeth to alter their appearance. They can address chips, gaps, crooked teeth, irregular sizing, and deep discoloration to create a beautiful, uniform smile.

Crowns repair extensive tooth damage by encasing the entire tooth, while dental veneers only cover the front part of your teeth. Veneers are a more conservative option that preserves more of your natural tooth structure and are ideal for cosmetic improvements rather than structural repair.

The process typically requires two to three appointments. After your initial consultation and tooth preparation, impressions are sent to the lab for approximately two to three weeks. During this time, temporary restorations protect your teeth. At your final appointment, Dr. Kahng inspects the fit and bonds your permanent veneers.

Porcelain veneers typically range from $1,300 to $3,200 per tooth, depending on case complexity, the expertise of your dentist, and the quality of ceramic used. During your free virtual consultation, Dr. Kahng will provide a personalized estimate based on your specific needs.

Dental veneers can last 10–15 years, although we have seen patients with veneers lasting up to 20 years. Longevity depends on proper care, including regular brushing, flossing, and dental check-ups.

Maintaining veneers is simple and mirrors natural tooth care: brush and floss daily, avoid biting hard objects (like ice or pens), attend bi-annual dental appointments, and wear a mouthguard if you play sports or grind your teeth at night.

Minor chips can sometimes be smoothed and polished for restoration. However, broken or significantly cracked veneers will need to be replaced. Dr. Kahng can assess the damage and recommend the best course of action.

Ideal candidates have healthy enamel and are free from cavities, decay, and gum disease. The best way to find out if veneers are right for you is to schedule a consultation with Dr. Kahng, who will evaluate your dental health and discuss your goals.
